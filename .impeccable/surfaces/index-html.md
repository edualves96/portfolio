## Direction contract

THESIS: The category-standard developer portfolio, chosen on purpose and executed at Linear and rauno.me craft: dark ground, sharp type, quiet motion, the work itself in a bento of product tiles. It refuses the lazy version: equal icon cards, hero-metric rows, kicker labels, decorative glow.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black tinted ground, white type in Schibsted Grotesk at 400/500/600 (replaced Geist during the build: Geist is one of the most saturated faces of the category, and the quality bar asks for Linear-grade sharpness, not that face), lime (#c8ff2e) as the one accent that does jobs (primary action, live status, data highlights, selection, focus) and one full-bleed lime close. Hairline borders, 16-20px radii, real screenshots and small live UI fragments inside tiles; Fragment Mono only for data and labels.

STORY: The visitor learns in one viewport that Eduardo builds whole products end to end, sees the products working, sees the day job and how he uses AI with guardrails, believes he can ship theirs, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky nav (monogram, section links, PT/EN, lime "Let's talk"). Left-aligned headline at display scale with "whole products" in lime, one-line sub, lime primary CTA plus ghost "See the work" and an availability dot. The top edge of the bento grid (EZLuga large tile, FindYourClub, upcc.pt) visible below the fold line.

FORM: canon (category standard, user-chosen over the roll); seed:62f2511f076a272f. Signature interaction: a pointer-tracked spotlight that lights tile borders and surfaces across the whole bento grid; tiles carry live fragments (ZÉ chat plays on view, HomeControl toggles respond). Motion grammar: expo ease-out reveals from blur and 16px rise, staggered once per section, headline words rise on load; reduced motion shows everything static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: custom domain, portrait photo, CV download.
