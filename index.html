AI, with guardrails

I use AI two ways: as a daily development partner, and as a part inside the products I build. In both, cost, privacy and a human yes come first.

  1. Context first

    Conventions, architecture and gotchas are written down in each repo, so the assistant's output matches the codebase.

  2. Verify, don't trust

    Tests, mutation checks, property-based tests and end-to-end suites decide whether AI-written code is accepted.

  3. A human says yes

    I review and commit by hand, approve generated content before it ships, and design agents to confirm before they write.

  4. Cost and privacy are requirements

    Budgets, caps, consent and data minimisation are built in from day one, not added later.
